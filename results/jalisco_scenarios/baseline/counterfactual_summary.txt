DIRECT vs INDIRECT
  Bands with zero doses still benefit: vaccinating other bands
  lowers the force of infection reaching them via the contact
  matrix. Direct and indirect interact in a nonlinear model, so
  these are an attribution, not an exact accounting identity.

ESTIMAND CAVEAT
  'No campaign at all' extrapolates well outside the data. q was
  also estimated IN THE PRESENCE of the campaign, so holding it
  fixed while deleting the campaign partly deletes something
  already absorbed into q.
